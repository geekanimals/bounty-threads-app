Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Applies to www.bountythreads.geekcreativeagency.com and the Geek Influencer OS app

On this page: Privacy Policy · Data Deletion

This Privacy Policy explains how Geek Online Ventures Pvt Ltd (“Geek”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use www.bountythreads.geekcreativeagency.com and the Geek Influencer OS app (the “Platform”). We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

1. Data we collect

From creators

  • Name, phone number (WhatsApp), email, city / state, date of birth, gender and profile details you choose to add (categories, languages, content types, pets, etc.).
  • Instagram data you allow through Meta’s official login: username, profile picture, follower count, posts and insights.
  • Delivery address (to ship barter products), and campaign activity — applications, contracts you accept, content you submit and posts you publish.
  • For paid campaigns: bank account or UPI details, account-holder name and KYC details required by law (such as PAN), for paying your fees.

From brands and agencies

  • Business name, contact persons, email, phone, address, GSTIN and billing details; campaign briefs and approvals; Facebook / Instagram business data you choose to connect.
  • Payment information: the payment status and reference returned by Razorpay. We never receive or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking passwords — these are handled only by Razorpay.

Automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, approximate location, pages visited and time spent, collected through essential and (with your consent) analytics cookies.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and run your account, match creators with campaigns and run campaigns end to end.
  • To ship products, send contracts and campaign updates (in the app, by WhatsApp and by email), and report results to brands.
  • To raise bills, collect brand payments and make creator payouts through Razorpay, verify payout accounts, deduct taxes where required, and keep accounting and tax records.
  • To keep the Platform secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and comply with law.
  • To improve the Platform. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

  • Brands see a creator’s public profile (name, Instagram handle, followers, engagement, city, categories) and campaign content and results — never a creator’s phone, email, address, bank details or fee unless needed to deliver the campaign.
  • Razorpay (payment aggregator) — to process brand payments, verify bank / UPI accounts and send creator payouts. Razorpay handles this data under its own privacy policy and RBI / PCI-DSS rules.
  • Courier partners — the creator’s name, phone and address, only to deliver barter products.
  • Service providers that host and run the Platform for us (cloud hosting and database, WhatsApp and email delivery, Meta APIs), bound to use the data only for us.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Payments and financial data

All online payments are processed by Razorpay on its secure, PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure. For creator payouts we store only the bank account number / UPI ID, IFSC and account-holder name needed to pay you, encrypted and visible only to authorised Geek staff. You can update these details at any time in the app.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and secure. Analytics cookies are used only if you choose “All cookies” in our cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing cookies in your browser.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data while your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes above. Billing, payment and tax records are kept for the period required by law (generally 8 years). Other data is deleted or anonymised when you close your account.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access is restricted by role. Social-media and payout tokens are stored encrypted. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as required by law.

8. Your rights

  • Access, correct or update your personal data (most of it directly in the app).
  • Withdraw consent, disconnect Instagram / Facebook, or ask us to delete your account and data — see Data Deletion.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (below) or, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Data from Meta (Instagram and Facebook)

When you connect an Instagram professional account or a Facebook Page / Business account, we use Meta’s official APIs (Instagram API with Instagram Login, Facebook Login and Facebook Login for Business) and access only the permissions you approve on Meta’s screen — for example your profile and media (instagram_business_basic), insights (instagram_business_manage_insights, read_insights), comments and messages you choose to manage (instagram_business_manage_comments, instagram_business_manage_messages), publishing (instagram_business_content_publish, pages_manage_posts), your Pages (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_read_user_content, pages_manage_engagement), Business Manager (business_management) and ad accounts (ads_read, ads_management).

  • We use this data only to show your profile, media kit and insights, publish or manage content and campaigns you ask us to, and report campaign results to you and the brand you work with.
  • We do not sell Meta data, use it to build advertising profiles, or share it with anyone except as needed to provide these features.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted on our servers and are never shown in the browser.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the app or from your Instagram / Facebook settings (Apps and websites). To delete the data we obtained from Meta, follow our Data Deletion instructions.

10. What “personal data” means

Personal data (also called personally identifiable information) is any information that identifies or can be used to identify, contact or locate a person — such as name, address, phone number, email address, financial details and payment information. It does not include information collected anonymously or demographic information not connected to an identified individual.

11. Login and usage information

We use login information — including IP address, internet service provider and browser type — to analyse trends, administer and secure the Platform, understand how it is used and gather broad demographic information. This also keeps accounts safe (for example, signing you out after long inactivity).

12. Your choices

  • You can opt out of non-essential WhatsApp and email updates from the unsubscribe link in any email, by replying STOP on WhatsApp, or by writing to us. Messages essential to a campaign you joined (contracts, shipments, payouts) continue while that campaign runs.
  • You can correct inaccurate data directly in the app or by emailing us.
  • You can ask us to delete or deactivate your data. Because of backups and legal record-keeping, some residual information may be kept for a limited time, but it is functionally deleted and never used, sold or transferred again.

13. Third-party services and links

Payments are handled by Razorpay and connected accounts by Meta; their own privacy policies apply to the data they process. The Platform may also link to other websites (for example a brand’s shop or a creator’s profile). When you follow such a link you leave our Platform — please read that website’s privacy policy, as its practices may differ from ours.

14. Children

The Platform is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

15. Changes

We may update this Policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change could lead to disclosing personal data you previously asked us not to disclose, we will contact you first so you can prevent it; other material changes are notified on the Platform or by email.

16. Grievance officer and contact

For any question, request or complaint about your data, contact our Grievance Officer. We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.

Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to delete your data from Geek Influencer OS, operated by Geek Online Ventures Pvt Ltd, including any information we obtained from Instagram or Meta with your permission.

Option 1 — Disconnect Instagram

To remove the Instagram data we hold about you while keeping your account:

  1. Sign in to Geek Influencer OS and go to Settings → Integrations.
  2. Click Disconnect next to Instagram / Meta.

We then delete your stored Instagram access token and the cached Instagram profile, media, and insights associated with your account. You can also revoke our access directly in Instagram under Settings → Apps and Websites.

Option 2 — Delete your entire account

  1. Sign in and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, media kit, uploaded content, and all Instagram data and tokens associated with your account.

Option 3 — Email request

If you cannot access your account, email givebacktogurugram@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject line Delete my data. Please include your Instagram username so we can locate and remove the associated records.

What we delete: your Instagram access token, and the cached Instagram profile, media, captions, media URLs, and insights we stored; and, if you delete your account, your platform profile, media kit, and uploaded content.

Timeline: disconnection removes Instagram data immediately; full account deletion is completed within 30 days. We may retain limited records where required by law, in de-identified or minimal form.

Once deletion is complete, we will confirm by email if you made a request by email. This page also serves as our data-deletion instructions for the purposes of Meta’s Platform Terms.
