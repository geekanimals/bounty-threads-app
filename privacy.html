Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026 · Effective date: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Geek Online Ventures Pvt Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”), operator of the Geek Influencer OS platform (the “Service”), collects, uses, shares, and protects your information — including information we access from Instagram and Meta with your permission.

By creating an account or connecting your Instagram account, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Who we are

Geek Online Ventures Pvt Ltd is a company incorporated in India, with its registered office at [Registered Office Address], Gurugram, Haryana, India. For any privacy questions, contact us at givebacktogurugram@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

(a) Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored only as a securely hashed value by our authentication provider), organisation/agency name, and role.
  • Profile and content you add: creator details, campaigns, barter items, notes, and any media or captions you upload to publish.

(b) Information from Instagram / Meta (only with your authorisation)

When you choose to connect your Instagram Business or Creator account, you grant us permission — through Meta’s official login screen — to access:

  • Profile information: your Instagram user ID, username, name, biography, profile picture, website, follower count, and media count.
  • Media: your posts, reels, captions, media URLs, timestamps, and related metadata.
  • Insights and metrics: aggregated statistics such as reach, impressions, profile views, and engagement, used to build your media kit.
  • Content publishing: where you direct us to, the content (images, videos, captions) you create in the app to publish to your Instagram account on your behalf.

We access this data only through Meta’s approved Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) and only for the features you use. We do not collect your Instagram password.

(c) Information we collect automatically

  • Basic usage and device information (for example, log data and general activity within the Service) used to operate and secure the platform.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and display your media kit (profile, audience size, and performance metrics) inside the Service.
  • To let you publish content to your own Instagram account when you choose to.
  • To help agencies and brands you work with discover, evaluate, and collaborate with creators, and to run campaigns and barter/paid deals.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To communicate with you about your account and the Service.

4. Legal basis and consent

Where required (including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), we rely on your consent — which you provide when you connect your Instagram account and when you use each feature — as well as the performance of our contract with you and our legitimate interests in operating the Service. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Sections 7 and 8).

5. How we share information

  • With your organisation: if you join or are invited to an agency/brand workspace, members of that workspace can see the profile and campaign data associated with it.
  • With brands and partners you choose to collaborate with, for the purpose of the collaboration.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf under contract — for example our database and hosting provider (Supabase) and, for payments, our payment processor. They may only use the data to provide services to us.
  • For legal reasons if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use data obtained through Meta’s APIs for advertising or share it with any data broker.

6. Instagram and Meta data — specific commitments

Our access to and use of information from Instagram and Meta complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Specifically:

  • We use Instagram/Meta data only to provide and improve the features you use (your media kit, analytics, and publishing).
  • We store access tokens securely on our servers and never expose them in your browser.
  • You can revoke our access at any time from your Instagram settings (Settings → Apps and Websites) or by disconnecting inside the Service.
  • If you revoke access or delete your account, we delete the associated Instagram data and tokens as described in our Data Deletion policy.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you disconnect Instagram, we delete the associated tokens and cached Instagram data. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain it to comply with legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, disconnect Instagram or delete your account inside the Service, or email us at givebacktogurugram@gmail.com. See our Data Deletion page for the deletion process.

9. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, secure server-side storage of credentials and access tokens, and row-level access controls that isolate each organisation’s data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard measures.

10. Children

The Service is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a minor has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Your data may be processed and stored on servers operated by our infrastructure providers, which may be located outside your country. We take steps to ensure such transfers are protected under applicable law.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, notify you within the Service.
