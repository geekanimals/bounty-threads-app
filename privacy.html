Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Applies to www.bountythreads.geekcreativeagency.com and the Geek Influencer OS app

This Privacy Policy explains how Geek Online Ventures Pvt Ltd (“Geek”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use www.bountythreads.geekcreativeagency.com and the Geek Influencer OS app (the “Platform”). We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

1. Data we collect

From creators

  • Name, phone number (WhatsApp), email, city / state, date of birth, gender and profile details you choose to add (categories, languages, content types, pets, etc.).
  • Instagram data you allow through Meta’s official login: username, profile picture, follower count, posts and insights.
  • Delivery address (to ship barter products), and campaign activity — applications, contracts you accept, content you submit and posts you publish.
  • For paid campaigns: bank account or UPI details, account-holder name and KYC details required by law (such as PAN), for paying your fees.

From brands and agencies

  • Business name, contact persons, email, phone, address, GSTIN and billing details; campaign briefs and approvals; Facebook / Instagram business data you choose to connect.
  • Payment information: the payment status and reference returned by Razorpay. We never receive or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking passwords — these are handled only by Razorpay.

Automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, approximate location, pages visited and time spent, collected through essential and (with your consent) analytics cookies.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and run your account, match creators with campaigns and run campaigns end to end.
  • To ship products, send contracts and campaign updates (in the app, by WhatsApp and by email), and report results to brands.
  • To raise bills, collect brand payments and make creator payouts through Razorpay, verify payout accounts, deduct taxes where required, and keep accounting and tax records.
  • To keep the Platform secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and comply with law.
  • To improve the Platform. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

  • Brands see a creator’s public profile (name, Instagram handle, followers, engagement, city, categories) and campaign content and results — never a creator’s phone, email, address, bank details or fee unless needed to deliver the campaign.
  • Razorpay (payment aggregator) — to process brand payments, verify bank / UPI accounts and send creator payouts. Razorpay handles this data under its own privacy policy and RBI / PCI-DSS rules.
  • Courier partners — the creator’s name, phone and address, only to deliver barter products.
  • Service providers that host and run the Platform for us (cloud hosting and database, WhatsApp and email delivery, Meta APIs), bound to use the data only for us.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Payments and financial data

All online payments are processed by Razorpay on its secure, PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure. For creator payouts we store only the bank account number / UPI ID, IFSC and account-holder name needed to pay you, encrypted and visible only to authorised Geek staff. You can update these details at any time in the app.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and secure. Analytics cookies are used only if you choose “All cookies” in our cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing cookies in your browser.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data while your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes above. Billing, payment and tax records are kept for the period required by law (generally 8 years). Other data is deleted or anonymised when you close your account.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access is restricted by role. Social-media and payout tokens are stored encrypted. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a breach as required by law.

8. Your rights

  • Access, correct or update your personal data (most of it directly in the app).
  • Withdraw consent, disconnect Instagram / Facebook, or ask us to delete your account and data — see Data Deletion.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (below) or, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

The Platform is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; material changes will be notified on the Platform or by email.

11. Grievance officer and contact

For any question, request or complaint about your data, contact our Grievance Officer. We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days.
